FAQs

Does microblading hurt?

A topical numbing cream is applied before and during the procedure. Most clients describe only mild discomfort — many relax completely.

How long does healing take?

Surface healing takes about 7–14 days. The pigment softens and settles into its final shade over 4–6 weeks, which is why the perfecting touch‑up is scheduled 4–8 weeks after your first session.

How long do results last?

Typically 1–2 years depending on skin type, lifestyle and aftercare. An annual color refresh keeps your brows looking soft and defined.

How should I prepare?

Avoid alcohol, caffeine and blood thinners 24 hours before, and skip waxing/tweezing a week prior. Full pre‑care instructions are sent after booking.

healing journey

What to Expect While Healing

Understanding the natural 4-stage healing process gives you complete confidence from day one.

Days 1–3

Crisp & Defined

Brows look sharp and slightly bolder in color as fresh pigment settles into the skin.

Days 4–7

Gentle Flaking

Light, natural skin flaking begins. Keep brows clean and follow aftercare balms.

Days 8–14

Soft "Ghosting"

Color temporarily appears lighter as fresh epidermal skin regenerates over the pigment.

Weeks 4–6

True Healed Color

True natural shade and stroke definition blossom — ready for your perfecting touch-up.
